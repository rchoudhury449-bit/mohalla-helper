Privacy & consent

Mohalla Helper is a local-service directory prototype for Barpeta, Assam. This page explains the intended privacy approach while the directory is being prepared.

Provider information

A provider's name, service category, locality, working hours, languages and phone number should be published only after the provider has given consent and the directory owner has reviewed the details. Do not publish another person's number without permission.

Registration form status

The current ZIP has no connected server, admin email, WhatsApp admin number, or online registration endpoint. The provider registration screen can prepare a copyable summary on the device, but it does not submit or publish the details. A registration form and a contact for corrections/removal must be connected before public launch.

Verification and safety

A verification label should be applied only after the directory owner has actually checked the provider. A listing is not a guarantee of service quality. Customers should agree on rates and timing before work begins. Emergency numbers are provided for convenience; use the appropriate official emergency service.

Local storage and connectivity

The app may cache its application files for offline loading. If a published provider CSV is configured, the app may also cache that list in the browser on the device. Avoid adding sensitive personal information.

Contact and removal

The owner contact details have not yet been configured. Before launching publicly, add a real contact method and a clear process for corrections or removal requests.

This is a starter privacy notice, not legal advice. Review and update it to match the final data collection and hosting setup.
